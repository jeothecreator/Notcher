### What's new

- **Play your own ROMs.** Drag a NES, Game Boy or Game Boy Color ROM (or a zip) onto the notch and it plays right there. Esc saves your spot, battery saves are kept, and controllers work.
- **Library page** with the last frame of each game as its poster, and a Continue card on For You.
- **Three homebrew demo games** to try before adding your own: click *Try the demos* in the Library.
- Built-in emulators that pass the standard CPU test ROMs, running on their own thread.
- Nine new built-in games, a category launcher, a quit button in the notch, and four new achievements.
