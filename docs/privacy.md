# Notcher privacy policy

*Last updated: 3 October 2026*

Notcher doesn't collect, store or share any information about you.

- **Nothing leaves your Mac.** Notcher makes no network connections. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and the Mac App Store version runs in the App Sandbox without network access.
- **Your data stays local.** Scores, trophies, settings, game saves and your game library are stored on your Mac, inside Notcher's own folder.
- **Your game files.** When you add a game, Notcher copies that file into its library. It reads only the files you choose: by dragging them onto Notcher, picking them in the Open panel or opening them with Notcher from Finder.
- **Sharing is up to you.** If you share a score card, Notcher copies its image to the clipboard and opens the macOS share menu. It goes only where you send it.

Questions? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/jeothecreator/Notcher/issues).
